Neil Longman has built the company’s reputation on quality and service, with close attention to detail and a hands-on approach to general contracting.

Longman works with the finest architects in Orange County, building to exacting standards and bringing the visions of the owner, architect and designers together without seams. He is committed to delivering on time and, more often, ahead of schedule and under budget.

He stands behind each home with a “within reason” warranty that lasts through the original ownership’s tenure. Referrals and repeat business have followed.
